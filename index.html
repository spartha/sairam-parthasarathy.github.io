About

Building programs that connect discovery, clinical care, and population impact.

Dr. Parthasarathy is an academic physician and investigator whose work focuses on improving outcomes for patients with sleep and respiratory disorders. His research integrates clinical epidemiology, patient-centered outcomes, pragmatic trials, implementation science, digital health, and biomarker discovery.

He has led interdisciplinary programs in obstructive sleep apnea, Long COVID, sleep health equity, and care delivery innovation. His approach emphasizes rigorous science, multisite collaboration, stakeholder engagement, and the translation of evidence into real-world practice.

Research

Core areas of investigation

01

Sleep Apnea and Personalized Care

Comparative effectiveness, positive airway pressure adherence, alternatives to PAP, endotyping, cardiovascular risk, and patient-centered treatment selection with $14 Million in funding.

02

Long COVID and Recovery Biology

Sleepiness, fatigue, cognition, autonomic symptoms, clinical outcomes assessment, biomarkers, and therapeutic trials in post-acute sequelae of SARS-CoV-2 infection with $24.5 Million in funding of observational studies and clinical trials.

03

Pragmatic and Decentralized Trials

Large-scale comparative effectiveness studies embedded in real-world care, with emphasis on recruitment, implementation, equity, and scalable outcome measurement.

04

Health and Sleep Equity

Culturally informed care in various populations to address health related social needs with ~$10 Million in funding.

05

Digital Health and Data Science

Multimodal clinical data, remote monitoring, synthetic controls, target trial emulation, predictive analytics, and AI-enabled clinical research infrastructure with ~$1 Million in funding from NIH.

06

Training in Lung and Sleep Research

Advanced research training experiences and long-term mentoring for junior faculty and transitioning postdoctoral scientists who are committed to investigating lung and sleep disorders with ~$4.5 Million in NIH funding.

Selected Programs

Large-scale collaborative research

PCORI Pragmatic Trial

Comparative Sleep Apnea Treatment

A four-site, 2,400-participant pragmatic study comparing treatment strategies for obstructive sleep apnea with 12-month patient-centered follow-up.

  • University of Arizona lead site
  • Multi-institutional collaboration
  • Patient-centered and real-world outcomes

NIH RECOVER

Long COVID Clinical Research

Leadership in cohort and interventional research examining sleep, fatigue, cognition, function, and recovery in adults with Long COVID.

  • National clinical trial collaboration
  • Sleep and wake-promoting interventions
  • Clinical outcomes and biomarker science

Center Initiative

Sleep, Circadian & Neurosciences Research

An interdisciplinary platform for developing team science across medicine, neuroscience, engineering, behavioral health, and population health.

  • Annual scientific symposium
  • Cross-campus investigator network
  • Mentorship and program development
